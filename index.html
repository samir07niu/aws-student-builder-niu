About the group

A community for students who want to build with cloud.

We are the AWS Student Builder Group at Noida International University — a student-run community built around one idea: the best way to learn cloud is to use it. We meet, we learn the fundamentals, we build projects, and we share what we learn with the next student.

Whether you are writing your first line of code or already deploying projects, there is a place for you here. You do not need prior AWS experience — curiosity is enough to start.

What we do

Four ways to grow with us

Cloud fundamentals

Core AWS concepts explained simply — compute, storage, databases, and networking — with guided hands-on practice.

Hands-on workshops

Interactive presentations and build sessions on campus where you follow along and leave with something working.

Real projects

Build and showcase projects with your peers — the work that turns classroom knowledge into demonstrable skill.

Career preparation

Guidance on cloud career paths and certification routes, from fundamentals to associate-level goals.

FAQ

Common questions

Who can join the group?

Any student at Noida International University who is interested in cloud and building with AWS.

Do I need prior AWS experience?

No. The group is built for beginners as well as students who already build. You start from where you are.

Is joining free?

Yes — participation in this student community is free.

What happens at events?

Interactive presentations and hands-on sessions: short talks, live demos, and time to build alongside other students.

How do I stay updated?

Write to the group leader, or come to the next event — the team will point you to the group's current updates channel.
